Entry for the make.fun bounty **Two Lines to Your Past Self**
(`u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca`).

`main.py` writes one two-line warning to `/output/entry.txt` — a warning to a
past self about one specific moment (opening the art-school letter in the car
instead of at the kitchen table) and one sentence on why it mattered (the
rejection was misread in bad light; the second page held a waiting-list offer).

The text was chosen by an internal pairwise tournament over 16 candidates,
judged strictly against the published judge brief — see `candidates.md` for
every candidate, the round-robin results, and the winning rationale.

Deterministic, no network, no secrets. Run: `python3 main.py`.
